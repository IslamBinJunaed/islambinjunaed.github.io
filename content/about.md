I'm **Junaed Islam Bin Md**, a Master's student in the Department of Global IT Engineering at Kyungsung University in Busan, South Korea. My thesis research is on deep-learning image restoration, especially blind removal of mixed Gaussian and impulse noise from RGB images.

## What this blog is

**Forward Pass** is my lab notebook in public. When a new model, paper or tool makes a bold claim in computer vision, image processing or machine learning, I try to test it myself with small, controlled experiments:

- one sharp question per post
- predictions and scoring rules written down before running anything
- controls that change one thing at a time
- every claim checked with code, not by eye
- an honest section on what the results *don't* show

Negative results are welcome here. Sometimes the most useful finding is where something breaks.
