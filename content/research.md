## Current research

**Blind mixed-noise image denoising.** My Master's thesis at Kyungsung University focuses on removing a mixture of Gaussian and impulse noise from RGB images when the noise type and level are unknown. The longer-term direction is a single, unified model that can handle many kinds of real-world image noise.

## Interests

- Image restoration and enhancement: denoising, deblurring, super-resolution, low-light imaging
- Robustness of vision models under noise and degradation
- Vision foundation models and vision-language models
- Generative models for images: diffusion and flow matching
- Evaluation: how we measure whether a model really works

## How I run experiments

Most posts on this site follow the same recipe:

1. Take a recent claim from a paper, model release or tool.
2. Ask one sharp question about where it might break.
3. Write predictions and scoring rules **before** running anything.
4. Change one variable at a time, with proper baselines.
5. Verify every claim with code.
6. Report what the results show, and what they don't.

Experiments run on free cloud GPUs (Kaggle), so they are small by design and meant to be reproducible.
